About

I'm a senior research scientist working where AI/ML meets sensitive, real-world data. Over more than a decade at CNRS I've taken work from niche idea to widely used tool: field-testing recording hardware on four continents, supervising state-of-the-art open-source speech AI, building the datasets and annotation pipelines to train and benchmark models, and working through the legal and ethical side of recording children at home.

What I bring

  • Range across fields. I've worked across anthropology, linguistics, psychology, economics and speech technology, which lets me bring very different teams together on the same problem.
  • Getting things built. I scope projects, decide what to build, buy or hire for, and see the work through to tools other people use.
  • Responsible AI in practice. I've put data protection, licensing and ethics review into day-to-day operations, not just policy documents.
  • Leading people. I've led teams of up to 12, hired for 20+ technical, administrative and research roles, and written onboarding and training for each.
  • Clear communication. I explain complex results to ethics boards, policymakers, practitioners and community representatives.

Current focus

I'm moving from building tools for researchers to building AI and data products used by millions: products that are safe, fair and useful for very diverse users, including children and under-served language communities.
